# Intro:

- Located in Sakleshpur
- 4-5kms
- 3-4 hours recommended ( we did it MUCH MUCH faster )
- Trek entry is from 7 AM till 12 PM
- Trek exit is mandatory by 4 PM
- Meaning of word: Bull's hull
- Part of western ghats
- We got late, but that's why were able to trek without a single human around!
# Trek:
## Base camp:

- Just a house with a family selling food
- Mountain seems SO FAR OFF!
- Photo of base camp
## Officer house:

- We reached by 13:00
- Pleaded multiple times for entry
- He called his superior, we got lucky!
- We could keep bags at officer house
- He asks for mobile number
- Surprisingly they didn't take plastic count
- Photos of office house and camp start info boards
## First section, flat walk till foor of the mountain

- Water pump, small stream due to it
- Fallen down tree
- Photo of the walk
- Photo of the pump
- Photo of shade walk where Somyadeep said to use vivid colour mode
## Second section: walk up the mountain amongst amazing jungle

- Loads of two way paths
- Photos of mushroom trees
- Photo of exiting forest section and approaching grasslands
- Moss overgrown trees, include photos too
## Third section: walk through the grasslands

- Open section where we first see the road to peak
- Can see few surrounding mountains from here
- Super large blue winged butterfly
## Fourth section: super steep, V0 rock climbing section to the top

- SUPER steep section
- Towards the end it needs you to climb some rocks
-  Photos of rock from below and above which needs climb
## Top

- Top is just heaven, clouds blowing through our faces
- Loads of dragon flies, bees, butterflies and a bettle
- Views of surrounding mountains etc
- Some hills are dense forest covered, while others are grassland covered
- We spent half an hour here
- Try to look online names of hills you can see from here
## Loads of flowers

- Purple flowers everywhere on whole path
- All the flower photos
## End

- 45 mins for both up and down, that's it. Mentioned time is too overloaded
