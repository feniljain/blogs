My reasons:

- For having writings to look back upon: weeknotes, conference reviews, trip reviews
- To express my feelings: poems
- To practice more writing: weeknotes
- Share cool stuff: technical writing
- To influence others: claude heads
- ABOVE ALL! For having clarity of thoughts: forever goals blog
- To get the worm out of my brain
- To share with others

Further goals:

- Add a RSS only section of personal notes. Keep linkedinese style blogs on the site, things I want to keep public always and rest of them public, addresses issue highlighted by Saachi while exposing limited audience to an interface. Same is the case with my weeknotes right now
- Lot more introspective writing
- Lot more technical writing
	- Sharing big architectural style ideas which keep cooking up in my head
	- Regular articles about things I have been learning in work/on side projects
- Improving my writing
