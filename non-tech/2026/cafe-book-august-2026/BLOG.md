You with your thoughts, both waiting for that traffic light to go green. As partners, you start wandering together the backlog of your dopamine-pilled brain. Countless thoughts unthought, countless talks unheard. Most of the days its just a still lake reflecting back the embarrassing moments from your grade school, but somedays you find a guiding light, a guiding light which suddenly clears your brain and you pour out the best of you! Little moments of boredom spark gleaming creativity.

Few weeks ago, I visited a little cafe called [Wishlist](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YuutWEa8G1XbRLDt8). While my sugar craving brain was waiting for the cake and coffee, I came across a small notebook. A notebook filled with boredom, a notebook filled with creativity. A place without wifi access and a handy notebook? Best combination to pour your heart out. I kept flipping the pages, and at times, few pages looked back at me. This is a collection of few of those pages `:)`.
