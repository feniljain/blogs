<pre>
<em>
All for naught,
All for naught,
"PLEASE GET DISTRACTED", I thought.
This mind is a prison,
It's a reactor with fission.

After all I have dealt,
I fear this reactor would melt.
These aren't the cards I played,
This wasn't how the game was laid.

All was good, there seemed no bind,
What were you unable to find!?
Was there something wrong when I tried?
Or did you wake up on the wrong side?

Hopes were so high,
I looked down at my feet,
And there was the sky.
Huh, what is this? rain?
When did I start to cry?

Shit happens, stay strong,
You weren't in the wrong!
But...that was the strongest I fought,
And now I know, it was ALL FOR NAUGHT
</em>
