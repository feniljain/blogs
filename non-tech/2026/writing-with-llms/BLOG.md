**Prompts to improve writing**

- can you read BLOG.md and suggest me suggestions to improve: 1. my sentence structures, small edits no full paragraph rewrite, 2. unnecessary repeated words, 3. series of few words which can be collapsed into a single word. Do not make any edits, just suggest.
- Things to improve upon:

  • Clarity: Flag sentences where a reader may lose track of who “he,” “they,” or “this” refers to.

  • Flow: Point out abrupt jumps between talks or sections and suggest a short connecting phrase where it helps.

  • Sentence rhythm: Mark long sentences that would read better with one full stop, without rewriting the paragraph.

  • Consistency: Check names, capitalization, and terms such as “Wi-Fi” versus “WiFi” and “devroom” versus “devrooms.”

  • Accidental errors: Catch typos, missing articles, and tense changes that distract from the story.

  • Tone: Flag wording that may sound stronger than you intend, while leaving the call to soften it to you.
