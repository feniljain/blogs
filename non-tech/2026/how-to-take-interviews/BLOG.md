- Everyone talks about how to give interviews, very few talk about how to take them.
- This blog is not going to talk about DSA style interviews.
- What I look for?
	- Fundamental knowledge.
	- Humility to say one does not know about something.
	- Thinking ability from first principles.
	- Curiosity about computing in general.
- I really like when there is a healthy github link in a resume, more applicable to senior folks.
- Take them seriously, they can change someone's life.
- In case of doubt, ALWAYS give candidate benefit of doubt.
- Hussain Nasser's idea of picking one topic and going deep into it.
- I try to steer conversations towards async cause it is tricky and there a bunch of things to explore.
- Prepare more than interviewee if needed.
	- I check resume a week before, analyze how much preparation time I would need. I block that time in calendar before the interview.
	- Even if the resume is straight-forward, I try to keep at least 30 minutes to go through person's resume plus profile in detail.
	- There have been times I was more prepared than the interviewee 🫠.
- You have to be competent to take a good interview! Strive for it. No one should be able to bullshit you, fundamentals are the same and enough for checking intuition.
- Keep a question bank of general questions ready for each job role you would be hiring for.
