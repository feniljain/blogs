<pre>
<em>
Why is water so yielding?
Flows where the earth takes
Morphs like the earth shapes

Shines with the wrath of sun
Calms with the hearth of moon
Weeps when the rail falls
Boils when the summer hauls

Absent with self image
Alive reflecting everyone's own page
Reflecting back their soul
Exposing them naked whole

Why don't you fight?
You can be YOU, you have the right!
Don't mind misstep on this pebble
Rise up, lead the rebel!
